About Me.

I'm Prakhar Katyayan, a Full Stack Developer building the web infrastructure of science at CERN, Geneva. By day, I keep the digital nervous system of the world's largest particle physics laboratory running: managing a fleet of 1,200+ websites, leading a major platform migration from Drupal to WordPress, shipping CI/CD pipelines, and working across OpenShift and Kubernetes to make sure the internet of particle physics stays online while the universe is being decoded a few hundred metres underground.
Before joining CERN's Web Team full-time, I contributed to the United Nations CITES digital platform. I hold a B.Tech in Networking & Cyber Security, and I'm currently pursuing my Master's degree simultaneously in Data Science - AI & ML from the University of Liverpool.
Outside of screens, you'll find me in Geneva wondering what the Higgs boson is up to, and occasionally remembering that I live in one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
